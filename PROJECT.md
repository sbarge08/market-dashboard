# AlphaSight

## Project Name

AlphaSight

## Meaning

Alpha = investment returns above a benchmark.

Sight = visibility, insight, and understanding.

## Elevator Pitch

AlphaSight is an AI-powered market intelligence dashboard that combines live stock prices, technical indicators, news sentiment, and AI-generated summaries into one modern interface.
## Target Users

AlphaSight is designed for:

- Beginner investors
- Finance students
- Computer science recruiters
- People researching companies
## Problem

Stock research requires checking multiple websites.

Users often need one website for:
- prices
- charts
- company information
- news
- analysis

AlphaSight combines these into one dashboard.
## Solution

A user enters a stock ticker.

AlphaSight retrieves financial data, calculates important metrics, displays charts, analyzes news sentiment, and creates an AI-generated market summary.
